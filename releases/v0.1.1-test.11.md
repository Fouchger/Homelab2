## 🎯 Release summary

Readiness was incorrectly blocking on deferred Ansible playbooks even though only the immediate LXC runtime definitions are required to mark the service as ready. This removes the Ansible paths from the readiness gate, keeps the LXC validation intact, and updates the unit test to cover the corrected behavior.
