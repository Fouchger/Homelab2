## 🎯 Release summary

Replace deprecated injected facts in the common role with ansible_facts["os_family"] and ansible_facts["service_mgr"]. Make the loki role discover its primary group by running `id -g loki` and use that value for file/group ownership instead of assuming a literal 'loki' group. Update unit tests to check for the gid discovery and the new facts usage and to ensure the role no longer hardcodes group: loki.
